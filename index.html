HALFWAY.

Privacy Policy · Last updated 20 October 2026

HALFWAY is a workout timer. It is built to work without knowing anything about you.

What we collect

Nothing. HALFWAY has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking and no adverts. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, and the app does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, workouts, presets, theme, streaks and workout history are saved only on your device, inside the app. They never leave it. Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, HALFWAY schedules them on your phone to tell you when a round changes or to remind you to train. They are created on your device and are not sent through any server.

Purchases

HALFWAY+ is a one-off purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to the purchase: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Other apps

If you choose to open another app from HALFWAY, such as a music app, that app’s own privacy policy applies.

Children

HALFWAY does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
